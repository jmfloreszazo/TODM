# Call for independent evaluation — TODM

**Can independently governed test oracles detect defects missed by tests generated alongside AI-produced software?**

TODM v1.5.0 is a proposed methodology accompanied by a reproducible **synthetic** Python/.NET 10 research demonstration.

Researchers in software testing, formal methods, model-based testing, and AI-assisted software engineering are invited to **reproduce, falsify, or compare** the approach. Negative, inconclusive, and positive findings are equally welcome. Participation does not imply endorsement.

- Artifact and DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23725201
- Source: https://github.com/jmfloreszazo/TODM
- Protocol: [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md)
- Report template: [RESULT_TEMPLATE.md](RESULT_TEMPLATE.md)

Begin with a reproduction attempt and open an issue documenting your environment, commands, and observed discrepancies. Independent validation has not yet been established.
